Islands & Guesthouses

Resort islands and local islands offer very different trips.

Pick your style

Resort islands

One resort per island. Private, polished and expensive. Alcohol and swimwear are fine everywhere.

Local islands

Real villages with guesthouses, cafés and public beaches. Cheaper, and you meet residents.

Local etiquette

Islam is the state religion. Dress modestly in villages. Alcohol is only served at resorts and on liveaboards. Many local islands have a designated bikini beach.
