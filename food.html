Food

Fish, coconut and chili, with a strong tea-time tradition.

Local dishes

Mas huni

Shredded tuna with coconut, onion and chili. A breakfast staple eaten with flatbread (roshi).

Garudhiya

A clear tuna broth served with rice, lime and chili.

Hedhikaa

Short eats such as fried fish balls and pastries, served with tea.
