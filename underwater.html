Underwater

The reefs are the reason most people come.

What you can see

Snorkeling

Many house reefs are a short swim from shore. Wear reef-safe sun protection.

Diving

Channels, called kandu, bring sharks, eagle rays and big fish.

Reef rules

Never touch or stand on coral, never feed fish, and keep distance from turtles and sharks.
