Travel Tips

Before you pack, here are some tips.

December to April is the dry season. May to November is wetter, but cheaper, and good for manta rays.

Many nationalities get a free 30-day visa on arrival. Check your passport rules before booking.

Reef-safe sunscreen, a rash guard, modest clothes for village visits, and a dry bag for boat trips.
